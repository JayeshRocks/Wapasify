The return journey

Three steps, more peace of mind

01

Report or search

Share a clear description, category, and broad campus area. Keep personal details and secret ownership clues out of the public post.

02

Verify privately

If something looks like yours, answer a private ownership question. A match suggestion is a starting point, never proof by itself.

03

Collect safely

Coordinate through the platform and collect through a campus-approved desk or staffed point. No public phone numbers or private meetups.
